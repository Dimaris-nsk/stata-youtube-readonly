STATA YouTube Readonly

About Privacy Terms

About

STATA YouTube Readonly uses YouTube API Services to help the channel owner collect and review channel and video metadata, YouTube Analytics reports, and available revenue data. It also supports locally imported YouTube Studio exports.

The tool provides a local record of source reports, SQL analysis, and text and semantic search. It requests no YouTube write scopes and does not upload, edit, publish, or delete YouTube content.

It is a personal tool operated for the channel owner, not a public service for connecting other people's accounts.

Privacy

Data accessed and its purpose

With the owner's authorization, the tool reads identifiers and metadata for accessible channels and videos, analytics measurements, and revenue reports where access is available. This information supports the owner's report collection, comparisons, calculations, and searches.

Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Local storage and processing

Source files, the database, and the search model are stored on the owner's Mac. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in macOS Keychain. Search embeddings are generated locally; texts are not sent to an external service for vectorization.

Owner-directed use of AI services

The owner also uses AI tools such as Codex and Claude. When the owner requests analysis, selected data may be sent to and processed by the AI services the owner chooses. The owner controls these recipients and the analysis requested. This is distinct from local embedding generation.

Such sharing is limited to the requested analysis with the owner's authorization. It is not a sale of data, advertising use, or public publication. AI services operate under their own terms and the owner's account settings; this notice makes no claims about those providers' retention or model-training policies.

Limits on use

The tool does not train AI models on Google user data, sell that data, or use it for advertising, credit scoring, or lending decisions. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This page does not imply Google approval or verification.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

API-derived data follows the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. Authorized Analytics, Reporting, and statistical data may be retained while needed for the owner's agreed purposes, with authorization checked at least every 30 days. Where applicable, the continued existence of the associated videos is also checked. Other API metadata is refreshed or deleted within 30 calendar days.

The owner can revoke access through Google Account permissions. This stops new authorized requests. Data obtained under the revoked consent, including associated local copies and derivatives, must then be deleted as soon as possible and within 30 calendar days. A deletion request made directly to the operator is handled as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days. Temporary network errors are distinguished from revoked authorization.

To request deletion, contact admin@masterskaya.com.co and identify the account, reports, or periods involved. The scope includes source files, database records, derived indexes, and associated backups. Deleting local copies does not delete the original content on YouTube. Independently imported Studio exports are distinguished from data collected through the APIs and remain under the owner's local retention instructions. Copies previously sent to an AI service must also be managed through that service's controls.

Contact

For questions about access, privacy, or deletion, email admin@masterskaya.com.co.

Terms

  • By using this client, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Use is limited to the channel owner's personal analysis of data they are authorized to access.
  • YouTube reports may be delayed, revised, incomplete, or unavailable. Report periods, filters, and coverage should be checked before comparing or combining results.
  • Local calculations and AI-assisted interpretations are analytical aids. The owner should verify important conclusions against the source reports.
  • The owner can stop using the tool and revoke API access at any time. Local deletion is handled as described in Privacy.
